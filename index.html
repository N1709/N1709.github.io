About

Bridging low-level system architecture and game programming to build deeply optimized software.

I am a dedicated student and solo developer specializing in Android Operating System (AOSP) development and game development. As the founder of AetheriaOS, a custom open-source Android ROM project, I have extensive hands-on experience in firmware customization, system package management, and device-tree modifications.

I am constantly exploring new technologies and am eager to collaborate on challenging open-source or software engineering projects, from cross-chipset firmware ports and interactive game loops to robust web back-end systems.
